Donnie Grance

Booked Monday, June 1, 2026

Presumption of innocence. This is a public-record booking report. Donnie Grance has been charged but not convicted. All charges are allegations and the accused is presumed innocent until proven guilty in a court of law.

Donnie Grance was booked into the Carroll County jail on June 1, 2026, on a charge of failure to appear probation violation for a fingerprintable charge. The violation was classified as a misdemeanor and was alleged to have occurred when the terms of Grance's probation were altered.

Failure to appear probation violations are misdemeanor-level offenses in Georgia that occur when a probationer does not comply with altered probation conditions or fails to appear at required probation meetings or court dates. Violations of probation terms can result in additional court proceedings and potential modification of sentencing, including possible incarceration, depending on the severity of the violation and the offender's history.
